 Build a Specialized Subagent

Move from reusable skills to autonomous, specialized workers. Identify a part of your project that deserves its own subagent with a clear responsibility, bounded context, and defined output.

What you need to do

·        Identify a task that would benefit from being handled independently.

·        Explain why it needs a subagent rather than a normal prompt or skill. Consider whether it requires independent reasoning, dedicated context, or independent operation.

·        Define the subagent's purpose, responsibilities, context, tools, inputs, outputs, constraints, and calling conditions.

·        Build and test the subagent and connect it to your project where appropriate.

Submission

·        Required: Working subagent package.

·        Required: GitHub repository or individual implementation files.

·        Required: Brief explanation of what the subagent does, why it needs to be a subagent, when it is called, and what context it receives.

Optional: Loom walkthrough and example inputs/outputs.
